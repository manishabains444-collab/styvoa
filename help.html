Shopping & service information

Delivery and availability

Styvoa sells directly to customers in India and may arrange products after an order is received. Availability must be confirmed before acceptance. The standard delivery estimate is 3–7 days, subject to the destination PIN code, availability and courier confirmation. Check the applicable delivery charge and estimate before paying. If a product cannot be supplied, contact support to arrange a resolution or refund.

Cancellation, return and refund

Request cancellation before shipment using My Account or contact support. If a product is damaged, defective, incorrect, materially different from its description or delivered late, contact Styvoa promptly. Your applicable statutory rights are unaffected. Return instructions, pickup or shipping arrangements and any applicable costs will be communicated before return. Approved refunds are processed manually and recorded against the order after funds are sent. No refund is implied by a request alone.

Design services

Single Room Design: ₹499, standard 3-day delivery and one included revision. Whole Home Design: ₹1,999, standard 5-day delivery and two included revisions. Product requests are free. Scope and timing should be confirmed before paying; products shown in a design are purchased separately.

Privacy and account information

We use the information you submit to fulfil orders, verify payments, prepare designs, manage support requests and communicate with you. Order and request history are linked to your Google or guest account. Payment is made through UPI or bank transfer, not by entering card credentials on this website. Optional browsing analytics can be disabled. For questions about your information, contact support.

Complaints and escalation

Submit a support request below and keep its reference. You can download a copy of the recorded complaint and review replies in this account. If unresolved, you can also contact India's National Consumer Helpline.
