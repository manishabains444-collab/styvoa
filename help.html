Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?Standard estimate is 3–7 days, subject to availability and PIN code.
How do I pay?UPI or bank transfer. Cash on delivery is not available. Payment is verified before dispatch.
How do I track my order?Open My Account to see order status and courier details when available.
How can I cancel or return an order?Use the request option in My Account. Requests are reviewed by Styvoa; approval is not automatic.
How do I request a room design?Choose Single Room Design or Whole Home Design on the homepage.
